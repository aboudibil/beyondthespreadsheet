The concept

What CPAF v6 is, and how it thinks.

A reasoning instrument for public policy based on complexity — it couples several analytical lenses at runtime to show not just what a policy does, but why.

Policy in its full scope

A policy is a state intervention, not only a fix.

A public policy is one of the ways a state intervenes in its environment to pursue goals, express priorities, and steer change. So CPAF reads a policy's outcomes against that intent, not only against whether a problem shrank — exactly the value a single efficiency lens cannot see.

What it is not

A deliberation aid, not an oracle.

  • ✕ Not a forecaster — it shows what can happen and why.
  • ✕ Not an optimiser — it opens a debate, not closes it.
  • ✕ Not a data product — its structure is the point.
